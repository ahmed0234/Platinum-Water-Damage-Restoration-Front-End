How soon can you start water damage restoration?

The sooner water damage is addressed, the better. Call us with your location and a description of the damage. We'll check our availability, discuss the next steps, and let you know when our team can schedule an assessment.

What should I do while I wait?

Keep people and pets out of the affected area. If you can reach the water shutoff safely, stop the source. Don’t enter standing water near electrical equipment or walk beneath a sagging ceiling. Call us and explain what you’re seeing. If there is an immediate danger, contact emergency services.

Can I just dry it with a few fans?

Surface drying may leave moisture under flooring or inside walls. A professional assessment checks affected materials and helps determine what equipment and repairs are needed. We use moisture readings to follow the drying progress.

How much does water damage restoration cost?

It depends on the area affected, the materials involved, the water conditions and the repairs needed. An assessment helps establish the scope. Call us to discuss your situation and the next step.

Will you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We document the damage and restoration work and coordinate with your adjuster. Your insurer determines coverage based on your policy and the cause of the loss.

How long does drying take?

The timeline depends on how much water is present and which materials are affected. We check moisture readings during drying and use those results to determine when the structure is ready for the next step.

Do you handle repairs after the cleanup?

Yes. Platinum provides reconstruction as well as water mitigation, including affected flooring, drywall, trim and paint. We’ll discuss the repair scope after assessing the damage.

Do you restore commercial properties?

Yes. We provide water damage mitigation and reconstruction for commercial and multifamily properties as well as homes. Call with your property type, location and the extent of the damage.
